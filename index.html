A FEW WORDS FROM MY HEART
♥

Dear Amma & Nana,

If I could give you one gift, it would be the chance to see yourselves through my eyes.

You would see two incredible people whose love has shaped my world in more ways than words could ever explain. You have given me more than a home. You have given me a sense of belonging, the courage to dream, and the strength to keep going when life becomes difficult.

Amma, thank you for your endless care, your warmth, and all the little things you do without expecting anything in return. Your love has a way of making even the hardest days feel lighter.

Nana, thank you for your guidance, your hard work, your support, and the values you teach me through your actions. You have shown me that love is not only something we say; it is something we do every single day.

I know there have been sacrifices, worries, and difficult days that I may never fully understand. But I hope you know that none of your efforts are wasted. Every lesson, every prayer, every moment you stood beside me means more than I can explain.

As your son, one of my greatest wishes is to see you both happy, healthy, peaceful, and smiling together for many, many years. I hope life gives back to you all the kindness you have given to our family.

Happy anniversary to the two people who will always be my first home and my forever inspiration. May your story continue to grow more beautiful with every passing year.

I love you both more than these words can ever say.

Forever your little one, Your loving son ♥
